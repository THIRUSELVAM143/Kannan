## 1. Project Overview

The strongest evidence is a steel-framed building/shed with roof trusses, secondary roof members, profiled sheet roofing, and large light-colored flat wall panels. Workers are shown handling/fitting panels and finishing joints. A smaller panel-clad structure is also shown. Separate finished-interior photographs show doors, window assemblies, counters, kitchen cabinets, wardrobes, and decorative wall/TV units. The photos do not establish that all interior examples are part of the same building or contract.

Use the cautious description **steel-framed, panelized construction**. The imagery does not establish whether the panels were factory-made, whether a complete volumetric module was fabricated off-site, or the brand/composition of the boards. Do not identify the roofing as V-Panel or R-Panel, and do not call the wall panels cement board, fibre-cement board, or insulated sandwich panels without labels/submittals.

## 7. Quality Inspection Checklist

1. Obtain approved structural drawings, calculations, material submittals, panel/roof datasheets, and applicable local code requirements.
2. Verify foundation/anchor locations, steel member identification, plumb/alignment, temporary bracing, and specified connection inspections.
3. Confirm actual roofing profile and installation manual; inspect support spacing, sheet condition, laps, fasteners, closures, flashing, penetrations, drainage, and leak performance.
4. Identify wall-panel product and exposure rating; inspect support/edge support, fixings, joints, opening details, base/top connections, and weather barrier/sealant where specified.
5. Confirm jointing materials are compatible with the panel and exposure; inspect for cracks, gaps, debonding, unevenness, and adequate cure before coating.
6. Check doors/windows for square/plumb frames, anchorage, operating clearances, hardware, glazing condition, seals, and water drainage.
7. For cabinetry, verify backing/anchors, level, alignment, movement, countertop support, edge finish, and moisture protection.
8. Have qualified persons inspect/test electrical work before concealment and at commissioning. The reviewed photos do not document those tests.
9. Record nonconformances and close-out photographs by location; do not accept a photo-only check as engineering certification.
